About the shop

A small room with a big machine

We opened in the spring of 2019 with one grinder, a second-hand lever machine and a box of mismatched cups from a charity shop. The room used to belong to a picture framer, which is why the windows are so wide and why the counter still sits slightly crooked.

Seven years on, not much has changed. Every shot is weighed, the menu is still chalk, and there is still a jar of biscuits for the dogs that stop at the door. Twelve seats inside, three small tables outside when the weather behaves.
